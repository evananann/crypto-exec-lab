Decision and limits

Use discrepancies to test the execution approximation. Do not treat agreement with a demo engine as production fill calibration or evidence of profitable cross-venue trading. This short batch cannot establish statistical reliability. Actual submitted exit quantities are conditioned on actual entry fills. Receive-time snapshots cannot identify the exact exchange book at wire arrival; local HTTP round trips include client, proxy and server work.

Every attempt is retained. A failure stops later attempts rather than replacing it. Private order IDs, balances and credentials are excluded from this page.
