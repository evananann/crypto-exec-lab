4. What the demo does not establish

Three sequential round trips in one short regime do not estimate a slippage distribution, tail probability, production fill rate or statistical trading edge. REST quotes miss intervening book changes. Exit quantities are conditioned on actual entries. Software receive clocks and ~313–331ms local HTTP round trips cannot validate 50ms one-way wire latency or matching-engine book state.
