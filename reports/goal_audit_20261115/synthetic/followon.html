What is not established?

The cells and bootstrap intervals are generated-data checks. There is no empirical alpha, causal exchange leadership, calibrated latency, capacity estimate, adjusted p-value or deployable winner. Constant-leader controls have no policy contrast and no interval. Read the separate draft experiment and enrollment requirements.
