What is established, and what is not

The released observations do not clear the modeled entry-fee hurdle. All 22 recorded daily means are negative. This supports an unfavorable description of that subset; the frozen conclusion remains inconclusive. Millions of market events and overlapping signal labels are not millions of independent experiments.

The primary study does not compare OKX-only information with OKX plus Binance. That requires the separately frozen matched L/2 versus L/2+B/2 experiment on common eligible opportunities. Positive incremental value would still need positive absolute execution economics.

Independent execution evidence

A separate cash-spot batch completed three demo round trips, six fills and 55 concurrent quote snapshots. Quantities and native-currency cash balances reconciled. Four prices matched; one sell was about 0.108bps worse and one buy about 5.009bps better than snapshot replay. These are limited demo-engine observations, not perpetual or production calibration. Local HTTP round trips of roughly 313–331ms do not identify one-way wire arrival.
