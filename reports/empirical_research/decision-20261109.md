# Execution decision: abstain pending released evidence

Evidence checked November 9, 2026. **Decision: no live deployment.**
This is a completed review of research readiness, not a completed market-effect estimate.

## Question and evidence

Does a received Binance jump predict an economically useful response on OKX?
The registered primary tests entry-to-mid markout after a 50 ms assumed delay
and a 5 bps entry fee. It does not itself measure a fully closed trading return.

The source-bound successor registration plans 56 slots from October 25 through
November 21. Outcomes, including development results, remain gated until
November 22 at 00:00 UTC (08:00 Shanghai). The empirical publication gate was
checked and is closed. Both the 91-file scientific binding and five-file model
binding match. These checks do not establish that collection occurred; current
slot coverage and archive delivery were not audited in this review.

| Quantity | Finding today |
| --- | --- |
| Primary mean and interval | Unavailable; restricted outcomes not read |
| Incremental value over OKX-only policy | Unmeasured; requires a separate experiment |
| Executable net returns and capacity | Unavailable |
| Trading edge | Neither established nor disproved |
| Capital allocation | Zero live deployment on the strength of this study |

## Economic decision

Abstain because the required evidence is unavailable. Do not describe this as
proof that taker trading loses money. Positive entry-to-mid markout would still
need executable exit prices, both fees, liquidity constraints, residual inventory
and calibrated latency. Synthetic examples establish accounting mechanics only.
Obside's independent momentum agents supply no evidence about this hypothesis.

## What happens after release

The installed released-report workflow must authenticate original archives,
retain all missing and failed slots, and apply the original interpretation rule.
The empirical-note publisher then copies the original phase estimates, coverage,
uncertainty and decision without fitting a new estimator or selecting a favorable
rerun. Missing data may leave the final finding inconclusive.

A supported primary result would justify consideration of a separately specified
paper execution experiment; it would not authorize live capital. A negative or
inconclusive result will be reported without changing thresholds or the holdout.

The paired OKX-only / OKX-plus-Binance comparison and ledger reconciliation
pipeline discussed in chat are not implemented by this note.
