# Five-minute researcher and trader defense

Prepared October 22, 2026. Practice and feedback are **pending the candidate's
answers**. This pack records no interview score, oral performance or human review.

## Timed first attempt

Record or say an answer without notes. Use current verified facts and say
“unavailable” for empirical estimates, calibrated execution or capacity.

| Time | Prompt | Evidence to defend |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–1:00 | What is the proposed mechanism and falsifiable primary hypothesis? | Asynchronous price discovery; exact leader/follower, receive clock, trigger, delay, fee and horizon; identify a rival explanation |
| 1:00–2:00 | Why might apparent predictability fail economically? | Entry-net markout versus executable exit, actual-notional fees, spread, later movement and unfinished exposure |
| 2:00–3:00 | What is an independent observation? | Days versus overlapping signals, phase separation, missingness, shared venue/date dependence and limits of bootstrap precision |
| 3:00–4:00 | What have you measured, and what remains hypothetical? | Two infrastructure trials; synthetic cost and throughput examples; scientific outcomes/calibration still unavailable |
| 4:00–5:00 | What is the trading decision and what would change it? | Remain in research, original frozen interpretation, feasible liquidation, uncertainty and separately authorized execution calibration |

## Follow-up challenges

1. **Cost arithmetic.** Assume a 60,000 entry, 0.001 BTC, +2bps entry-net markout,
   5bps entry fee, zero exit spread/slippage/funding and a 5bps exit fee. Explain
   the implied 60,042 long horizon mid, 0.042 gross price gain, 0.03 entry fee,
   0.030021 exit fee and **−0.018021 USDT** net. Do not charge entry fees twice.
2. **Competing explanation.** Could collector transport delays or common news
   produce the same apparent lead? Explain a discriminating measurement and its
   limits. Distinguish a proposed diagnostic from a completed result.
3. **Positive result, weak evidence.** An observed mean is positive but days are
   missing and intervals are wide. State the original coverage decision and
   the economically relevant effects still compatible with the observations.
4. **Unfinished hedge.** Net BTC is zero across two venues. Identify remaining
   gross exposure, basis, collateral, funding, closing costs and failure risk.
5. **Sizing.** Why does multiplying the lab's quantity not establish capacity
   or justify Kelly sizing? Name missing fill, impact, tail and capital inputs.
6. **Negative result.** Give a useful conclusion without selecting a better
   venue, extending the sample or tuning the trigger after outcomes.

## Self-review rubric

For each timed minute, assign 0 for an unsupported/incorrect claim, 1 for the
main idea, 2 for the idea with concrete evidence, and 3 for evidence plus a
material limitation or counterexample. This is a suggested self-review scale,
not a recorded result or hiring prediction. Repeat the weakest answer with a
follow-up challenge; retain the first answer so improvement is observable.

Never substitute the synthetic 22.4-million-event benchmark for independent
market observations, collection health for alpha, or full-period precision for
untouched-holdout precision. A defensible result can be negative or inconclusive.
